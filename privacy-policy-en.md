# Movability — Privacy Policy

Oct 2, 2026 · @Vasilis

## 1. Who we are

Movability (movability.gr) rents mobility equipment in Athens, Greece. The service is operated by KOINIS IKE, VAT No EL999050083, Ipsilantou 22, 17778 Tavros, Attiki, Greece. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

For any privacy question or request, write to info@movability.gr.

This policy covers our website, bookings, customer accounts, the AI chat assistant, and our email and WhatsApp communication with you.

## 2. What personal data we collect

We collect only what we need to rent you equipment, deliver it and answer your questions.

| When you | What we collect |
| --- | --- |
| Make a booking | Name, email, phone number (including your WhatsApp number), delivery address or hotel name, rental dates, delivery time slot, delivery notes, the equipment booked, amounts paid, and any special requirements you choose to write |
| Create an account (optional) | Name, email, phone number, and a password (stored only as a one-way hash) or your Google sign-in identifier |
| Pay | Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. We keep the payment status, amounts and Stripe payment references |
| Contact us or send a partner enquiry | Name, email, phone number, subject and message |
| Chat with our AI assistant | The messages you type, the language, your browser's user-agent text, a salted one-way hash of your IP address (not the IP address itself) and timestamps |
| Message us on WhatsApp or by phone | Your number and the content of the conversation |
| Browse the site | Cookies and analytics data, as described in section 8 |

We also send you automated emails: booking confirmations, a review request after your rental, and a single reminder if you start a booking but do not complete payment.

**Health information.** The special requirements and delivery notes fields are optional free text. You do not need to tell us a diagnosis — describing your functional needs (for example "step-free access" or "narrow doorways") is enough for us to bring the right equipment. If you choose to provide information about your health or a disability, we ask for your explicit consent at the point you provide it, and we process that information solely to arrange and fulfil your rental. We never use it for marketing. You can withdraw your consent at any time at info@movability.gr; withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out before it.

We do not sell personal data.

## 4. Who we share it with

We share personal data with the service providers and other recipients below. Depending on the service and the processing activity, they may act as processors on our instructions or as separate controllers. Our own delivery team and staff see only what they need (name, phone, address, equipment).

| Provider | What it does for us | Your data it handles |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Vercel | Hosts the website | Technical request data |

We may also disclose data to our accountant and to authorities where the law requires it.

If you post a review on Google, that review is published by Google under Google's own policy, not ours.

## 5. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Some of our providers are based in the United States or process data there. When your data leaves the EU/EEA we rely on an EU adequacy decision (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified providers) or on Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards at info@movability.gr.

## 6. How long we keep data

| Data | How long |
| --- | --- |
| AI chat conversations | 90 days, then deleted automatically |
| Booking and payment records required for accounting and tax | 5 years from the end of the year in which the related tax-filing obligation arises, or longer where the tax assessment period remains open, an audit or dispute is ongoing, or another legal rule requires it |
| Operational booking details (delivery notes, addresses, time slots) | 2 years after the rental ends |
| Health information you provided for a booking | 60 days after the rental ends |
| Contact and partner enquiries | 2 years |
| Bookings started but not paid | 6 months |
| Customer account | Deleted after 3 years of inactivity (we warn you first), or earlier on your request |
| Analytics data | Per our Google Analytics retention setting (14 months) |

When a retention period ends we delete or anonymise the data, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

## 7. The AI chat assistant

Our website has a chat assistant powered by artificial intelligence from Anthropic. When you open it, we tell you clearly that you are chatting with an AI, not a human. It answers questions about our equipment, delivery and Athens. It cannot make bookings, confirm availability or give medical advice, and it does not make any decision that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you. A real person answers on WhatsApp.

**What we store:** the text of your messages, the language, your browser's user-agent text, a salted one-way hash of your IP address (we use it only to limit abuse and cannot recover your IP from it), timestamps and technical usage counts.

**Who sees it:** Anthropic processes the text of your messages on our behalf to produce the replies. Our team may read conversations to improve the service.

**How long:** conversations are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Please do not type health details, card numbers, passwords or ID numbers into the chat — the assistant does not need them and is instructed not to ask for them. If sensitive information is nevertheless sent, we do not use it for any purpose beyond answering you, and we delete conversations containing unnecessary sensitive information earlier than the standard 90 days, on review or on your request at info@movability.gr.

## 8. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies that store or access information on your device. Google Analytics helps us understand how the site is used (pages visited, device type, approximate location). Its cookies are set only if you accept them in our cookie banner, where Accept and Reject are offered equally, and you can change your choice at any time.

The site also keeps a few things in your browser's local storage so that it works: your language choice, your shopping cart, and whether you have closed the chat tip. These are needed to provide the features you asked for and are not used for tracking.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your personal data (access)
- correct data that is wrong (rectification)
- delete your data (erasure), where the law allows
- limit how we use it (restriction)
- give you your data in a portable format (portability)
- stop using it for our legitimate interests, including the booking reminder and review request (objection)
- stop relying on your consent, for example for analytics cookies or health information you wrote (withdrawal)

Write to info@movability.gr. We reply within one month. If you are not satisfied, you may lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr) or, where applicable, with the supervisory authority in the EU/EEA Member State of your habitual residence, place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement.

## 10. Security, children and changes

**Security.** Connections to our site are encrypted. Access to bookings is limited to our staff, and the database enforces access rules. Card payments go through Stripe. Where required by applicable data-protection law, we will notify the competent supervisory authority and affected individuals of a personal data breach.

**Children.** Bookings and customer accounts may only be created by persons aged 18 or over. Adults may rent equipment for a child in their care; any information about the child is processed only to fulfil that rental.

**Changes.** If we change this policy we will update the date below and, for material changes, tell you on the website.
